# 🛡️ Assura

> Modern Insurance Management Platform

---

## 📌 Overview

Assura is an enterprise-grade Insurance Management Platform designed to manage customers, insurance policies, premium payments, claims, and documents through a modern web interface.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Customer Management
- Policy Management
- Premium Tracking
- Claim Management
- Document Management
- Reports & Analytics

---
